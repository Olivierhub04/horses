How to Use the Site as a Shipper


Welcome to the online platform that connects shippers and carriers of horses. Whether you need to transport your horse for a show, a sale, a relocation, or any other reason, we are here to help you find the best carrier for your needs. We are currently a startup company, and we are working hard to grow our database of carriers across the US. That's why we are offering free access to the site for shippers. You can sign up for your free account and start posting the horses you need transported.


 


What to Do If You Don't Receive a Call?


We understand that finding a carrier for your horse can be a time-sensitive and stressful process. That's why we are constantly working to attract more carriers to the site and expand our coverage of the US markets. However, we also ask for your patience and understanding. As a new platform, we may not have enough carriers in your area or for your specific trip. If you don't receive a call from a carrier within a reasonable time, please don't give up on us.


 


We appreciate you giving us the opportunity to serve you and your horse. We hope that you will enjoy using the site and find it useful and convenient. We are always looking for ways to improve our platform and our service, so please feel free to share your suggestions, comments, or questions with us. Thank you for choosing the site, and happy shipping!
