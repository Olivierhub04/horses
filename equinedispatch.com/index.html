ABOUT US

Equine Dispatch: The Ultimate Platform for Horse Shipping

Equine Dispatch is a service that connects horse owners with carriers who can transport thier horses safely and efficiently.  Whether you need to move your horse across the state or across the country, Equine Dispatch can help find you the best carrier for your needs.

HOW IT WORKS

Shippers

We wanted to keep this process as simple as possible. After creating an account with us you can then begin posting all of your transport needs.

Become a Shipper
1

Post Your Horse(s)

Post your horse(s) on Equine Dispatch. You will need to provide some basic information such as the pickup location and date, dropoff location, horse information, and your desired price.

2

Wait for a Carrier

Wait for a carrier to contact you. You will receive notifications when a carrier is interested in your transport request. You can chat with the carrier to narrow down the specifics of the transport.

3

Deliver & Rate

The carrier will pick up your horse(s) from the agreed location and deliver them to the final destination. Once the transport is complete, you can rate and review the carrier.

HOW IT WORKS

Carriers

Grow your transport business by hauling horses on Equine Dispatch. Browse local or long-distance jobs, bid on routes that fit your schedule, and secure guaranteed payments through our escrow system.

Become a Carrier
1

Find Horse Shipments

Search open horse transport jobs along your preferred routes. Filter by location, trailer type, and shipment dates to find the perfect load.

2

Submit Quotes & Chat

Submit bid proposals directly to shippers. Chat securely to negotiate pricing, terms, and transport logistics.

3

Deliver & Get Paid

Pick up and safely haul the horse(s). Once delivery is confirmed, the escrow payment is released instantly to your account.

Equine Dispatch - Guaranteed Deposit System

Equine Dispatch’s Guranteed Deposit System is designed to be user-friendly and effective. The system collects the full deposit amount from the shipper, plus an additional 10%, and holds the deposited funds securely until the shipper authorizes their release. In the event that the carrier fails to show up or cancels the shipments, the shipper will receive a full refund of their deposit.

Layovers

Layovers: A Key Factor for Safe Horse Transport

How Equine Dispatch can help you find the best layover farms for your horses.

What are layovers and why are they important?

Layovers are stops that horse carriers make during long-distance trips to allow their horses to rest, stretch, eat, and sleep comfortably. Layovers are essential for the health and well-being of horses, as they prevent stress, dehydration, fatigue, and muscle stiffness that can result from prolonged confinement in a trailer. Layovers also benefit the carriers, as they can refresh themselves, check on their vehicles and equipment, and avoid driving when tired or in bad weather conditions. Layovers can also reduce the risk of accidents, injuries, and legal issues that can arise from transporting horses without adequate breaks.

How long and how often should layovers be?

The frequency and duration of layovers depend on several factors, such as the distance and duration of the trip, the number and condition of the horses, the weather and road conditions, and the availability and quality of layover facilities. However, some general guidelines are:

• A layover should be at least 4 hours long, preferably overnight, to allow the horses to rest and recover from the stress of travel.

• A layover should be scheduled every 8 to 12 hours of driving, or every 500 to 700 miles, whichever comes first.

• A layover should be planned in advance and booked with a reputable layover farm that can provide adequate facilities and care for the horses.

• A layover should be avoided in extreme temperatures, high altitudes, or areas with poor air quality, as these can affect the horses' respiratory and cardiovascular systems.

How can Equine Dispatch help you find the best layover farms?

Equine Dispatch is a platform that connects horse carriers with layover farms across the country. We understand the importance of layovers for safe horse transport, and we want to help you find the best layover farms for your horses.
